## How

1. Restate the question in one line and decide which numbers would answer it.
2. Search in parallel (`WebSearch`, standard mode; extended only for hard or very recent facts).
   Prefer primary sources: company releases, federations, annual reports. Aggregators (star or
   valuation trackers, "stats" sites) only as a fallback, labelled as such.
3. Mark everything: **fact (source)**, **estimate (how derived)**, or **assumption**. Give ranges,
   not false precision. Date the figures.
4. Separate the angles the owner asks for (e.g. customers vs. finances), then summarise with a
   clear recommendation and what would change it.
5. End with "How to check this": the cheapest real-world test (talking to clubs, a pilot, a price
   test) that beats more desk research.
6. Sources at the end as links. Offer to update `docs/market-analysis.md` when the result should
   be kept.
