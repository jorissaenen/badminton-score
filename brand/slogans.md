# Skoor slogans

The domain is **skoor.it**, and the `.it` is the joke: *score it* → *Skoor it*. Every slogan is
short enough for a screen at the venue, a sticker on a racket bag or a 6-second video.

## The main line

**Play it. Skoor it.** — *Speel het. Skoor het.*

The tagline under the logo, on the sign-in screen, the first slide on venue screens, the end card
of every video, the website's title. Always with the full stop: two short commands, like a coach.

## For commercials and social media

**About keeping score**
- One tap. Every point.
- Less counting. More playing.
- Your eyes on the shuttle, not on the score.
- The score, without the discussion. — *De score, zonder discussie.*
- Was that 14–12 or 12–14? Skoor knows.
- Click. Point. Next rally.

**About the club**
- From first serve to last drink.
- Plan it. Play it. Skoor it.
- Who's coming tonight? Skoor it.
- Your club night, live on every screen.

**About statistics and rivalry**
- Every point counts. Now you can count them all.
- Bragging rights, in black and white.
- You versus them. Since the first match.
- Settle it on the court. Skoor it forever.

**About sports** (badminton, table tennis, padel, tennis, squash, pickleball)
- Any racket. Any club. Skoor it.
- Shuttle, ball or paddle: Skoor it.

**For venues and sponsors** (the advertising slides on /link)
- Your logo, between every rally.
- Seen at every point.

## Using them

- Write **Skoor** with a capital S in sentences; the logo spells SK00R, text never does.
- *Skoor it* works as a verb for anything that is kept, planned or followed in the app: "Skoor
  your club night", "Skoor the final". Don't overdo it: once per ad or screen is enough.
- In Dutch, *Skoor het* reads naturally; for French and German later: *Skoor-le.* / *Skoor es.*
- Check before printing: the rivalry lines ("Bragging rights", "You versus them") fit social
  media; on screens in a club hall, the friendlier ones fit better.
